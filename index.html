About Us

A school shaped by its valley

My New School, Drugmulla sits in the heart of Kupwara — where the school day still opens with the sound of the assembly line, walnut trees along the boundary wall, and a teaching staff that has watched most of the village grow up. We hold to a simple aim: strong fundamentals, wide participation, and a school that families can rely on.
